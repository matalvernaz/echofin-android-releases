# EchoFin for Android

EchoFin is a Jellyfin app made for people who use a screen reader. It plays audiobooks, music, films and TV, and works well with TalkBack. It's the Android version of EchoFin on the iPhone. It's still a test version, so please tell me when something's off.

You need Android 8 or later (downloads need Android 11), and an account on a Jellyfin server. If I invited you, the app already offers my server when you sign in.

## Get it, and have it keep itself up to date

This uses Obtainium, a free app that installs EchoFin for you and then checks for new versions. On your phone:

1. [Download Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium/releases/latest/download/app-release.apk). It's about 90 MB, so use Wi-Fi if you can. Open the download and choose Install. If Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps, choose Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and choose Install.
2. [Add EchoFin to Obtainium](https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://app/{%22id%22:%22ca.cobd.echofin%22,%22url%22:%22https://github.com/matalvernaz/echofin-android-releases%22,%22author%22:%22matalvernaz%22,%22name%22:%22EchoFin%22}). Obtainium opens and shows what it's adding. Confirm it.
3. In Obtainium, choose EchoFin, then Install. Android asks once whether Obtainium may install apps. Allow it the same way as in step 1.

That's it. Obtainium tells you when there's a new version. If Obtainium installed your copy and your phone has Android 12 or later, it may install updates by itself. If Android asks you to confirm an update, that's normal. This setup is new, and I haven't checked every Obtainium screen with TalkBack, so if a step doesn't match what you hear, or an update doesn't turn up, tell me.

## Or just download it

[Download EchoFin](https://github.com/matalvernaz/echofin-android-releases/releases/latest/download/EchoFin.apk), open the download, and choose Install. For a new version, do the same again.

## Already have EchoFin from my Nextcloud link?

No need to remove it. Set things up the same way, and your sign-in and downloads stay put. The first update through Obtainium may ask you to confirm it.

## If something goes wrong

In EchoFin, go to Settings, then Diagnostics, and choose Send diagnostic log. Then tell me what happened and roughly when. Your sign-in keys are taken out of the log before it's sent.

## What's new

The app tells you after each update, and keeps every version's notes in Settings, under Build, What's new. They're also on the [releases page](https://github.com/matalvernaz/echofin-android-releases/releases).
